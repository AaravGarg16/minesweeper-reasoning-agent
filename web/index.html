Minesweeper Solver

Each revealed number counts the mines in the eight squares touching it. From those numbers this agent works out the exact chance that every hidden square holds a mine, then opens the safest one. Sometimes no square can be proved safe, and it has to gamble.

Safe Mine

The shade of a hidden square is its chance of holding a mine, worked out exactly. Solid green means proved safe, solid red means proved to be a mine. Squares too far from any number to say anything about are left grey, since every one of them carries the same odds. The blue outline marks the square it just opened.
